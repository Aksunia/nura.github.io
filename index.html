How Nura works

Intelligence that adapts to you.

Three core systems work together to reshape how information reaches your brain.

Adaptive Content

Information shaped to fit your mind.

Not everyone absorbs knowledge the same way. Nura restructures dense material into the format that works best for you — summaries, visual maps, interactive Q&A, or step-by-step walkthroughs.

The system learns from your interactions over time: which formats you engage with, where you slow down, and what you skip. Every session gets sharper.

Content Graph
Burnout Radar

We see the wall before you hit it.

Nura passively monitors signals that indicate rising cognitive load — typing cadence, scroll patterns, tab-switching frequency, and session duration. No surveys, no interruptions.

When it detects early signs of fatigue, it intervenes: slowing the pace, suggesting a break, or switching to lighter content. You stay in flow longer and recover faster.

Fatigue Monitor
Smart Scheduling

The right content at the right moment.

Nura maps your peak focus windows — the hours where deep learning sticks. It schedules demanding material for high-energy periods and reserves lighter review for the wind-down.

Combined with spaced repetition, it ensures you revisit concepts at the scientifically optimal interval. Nothing slips through the cracks, and nothing feels like a chore.
